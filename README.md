# GroundhogZ

A fairly irresponsible rodent rescue simulator. It's a native macOS game in Swift, rendered with Metal.

Groundhogs drop out of a crate and waddle mindlessly forward. You give individual hogs jobs (digging, punching through walls, stacking carrot stairs, holding brollies, exploding) so that enough of them reach the burrow before time runs out. There are **101 levels**: 8 hand-made ones and 93 that the game generates itself, and every level is checked by a built-in simulator to make sure it can be beaten.

## Download

Get `GroundhogZ.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/lydianb79/Groundhogz/releases/latest), unzip it and move `GroundhogZ.app` to Applications.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so the first time you open it, right-click the app, choose **Open**, then confirm. If macOS still refuses, run:

```bash
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/GroundhogZ.app
```

You need a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 13 or later. The game always starts in fullscreen; Ctrl-Cmd-F switches back to a window.

## Build from source

```bash
./build.sh
open build/GroundhogZ.app
```

You only need the Swift toolchain (Xcode or the Command Line Tools). There's no Xcode project and no asset files: the shaders compile at runtime, and every sprite, the font atlas, the app icon, all sounds and all music are generated by code.

## Skills

| Key | Skill | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SCRAMBLER | Climbs walls (permanent) |
| 2 | BROLLY | Opens an umbrella when falling (permanent) |
| 3 | KABOOM | 5-second fuse, then blows a crater (not in steel) |
| 4 | BOUNCER | Stands in sunglasses and turns everyone around |
| 5 | STACKER | Builds a 12-step staircase out of carrots |
| 6 | PUNCHER | Tunnels horizontally |
| 7 | PICKAXE | Tunnels diagonally down |
| 8 | DIGGER | Digs straight down |

A hog that falls more than 60 px without a brolly goes splat. Water is fatal. Steel can't be dug. You earn up to 3 stars per level.

## Controls

- Click a hog to give it the selected skill.
- `-` / `=` lower or raise the release rate.
- `P` / Space pauses. `F` turns fast-forward on or off.
- `N` twice nukes everything.
- `R` restarts the level.
- `M` turns the music on or off.
- `Esc` goes back (on the title screen, it quits).
- Arrow keys, A/D, the screen edges, the trackpad or the minimap scroll the view.
- On the title screen, `U` unlocks every level.

## What's in it

### Presentation

- **Splash screen:** fades in at night as the sun in sunglasses rises, a groundhog pops out of its hole and waves, and the logo drops in letter by letter. It flows straight into the title screen, which uses the same scene, logo and text styles; only the menu buttons slide in.
- **App icon:** painted at full resolution by code (`--icon`).
- **Level select:** 101 levels across pages, with stars and your best percentage. The briefing and results screens are parchment cards.
- **Transitions:** an iris wipe between screens.

### Graphics

Each frame is drawn in several Metal passes:

1. **HDR scene** at up to 3× the logical resolution:
   - **Sky:** a procedural gradient with a sun (or a moon and stars at night), sunbeams, shaded clouds, birds, and parallax mountains, hills and swaying forests that change with the season, plus drifting ground mist.
   - **Terrain shader** that reads the destructible pixel map:
     - smooth anti-aliased edges with bevel lighting
     - rock layers, pebbles and roots
     - grass with swaying blades and flowers, or snow caps in winter
     - riveted steel plates and carrot bricks
     - soft drop shadows onto the background
   - **Sprites:** groundhogs at 4× detail, drawn oversized and scaled down so the edges are smooth, with shading, glossy hard hats, blinking, cheeks and whiskers.
2. **Bloom and god rays:** a bright-pass, a blur, and sunbeams that streak across the screen and are blocked by terrain.
3. **Composite:**
   - water that refracts and reflects, with light patterns underneath and sparkles on the surface
   - dynamic lights (lanterns, burrow windows, miners' lamps, explosion flashes) over a darker ambient at night
   - seasonal weather (snow, falling leaves, petals, fireflies)
   - colour grading and a vignette
4. **UI:** wooden 9-slice panels and a rounded system font rendered with CoreText.

**Effects:**
- explosions with a fireball, a shockwave ring and tumbling rocks
- dirt chunks when digging
- dust puffs when hogs land
- confetti at the burrow
- smoke from the burrow chimney
- ghosts with halos for the fallen

### Sound

There is an offline synthesizer in `Synth.swift`:

- **Instruments:** band-limited oscillators, filters, FM bells and electric piano, plucked strings, pads, brass and drums.
- **Music:** an algorithmic composer writes a loop of about a minute for each season and one for the title. The title music starts on the splash screen.
- **Voices:** hogs babble in synthesized squeaks, and say "wheee", "uh-oh", "yay" and "ow".
- **Ambience** loops for each season: birds, wind, rustling leaves, and crickets and owls at night.
- **Playback:** sound effects are panned by where they happen on screen, everything has reverb, and the music cross-fades between screens.

## Developer tools

```bash
B=build/GroundhogZ.app/Contents/MacOS/GroundhogZ
$B --verify                                   # play every level's built-in solution headlessly
$B --sim --level 42                           # run one level's solution and print stats
$B --snapshot shot.png --level 30 --ticks 900 --solution --size 1728x1080
$B --snapshot splash.png --screen splash --t 3  # also: title, select, briefing, results
$B --atlas atlas.png                          # dump the sprite sheet
$B --icon icon.png                            # render the 1024 px app icon
```

You can also write your own `--do` rules: `digger@x200` fires when a hog is at x = 200, `@dN` sets the direction, `@tN` waits until tick N, `@nN` sets how many times it fires, `@hN` targets hog id N, and `@sN` waits until N hogs are saved.

## How the generated levels work

`LevelGen.swift` builds each of the 93 levels from a seed as a chain of obstacles:

- walls (Puncher)
- water and pits (Stacker)
- ledges and steel fences (Stacker stairs)
- digging through a floor where a steel wall blocks the way (Digger)
- deadly cliffs (Pickaxe tunnel)
- long drops (Brolly for everyone)
- sheer cliffs (Scrambler for everyone)

Difficulty rises across the levels: more obstacles, more hogs, faster release, higher rescue targets and fewer spare skills. About half the levels are mirrored.

Each level also gets a scripted solution: one hog clears the route while a Bouncer on a steel pad holds back the crowd, then a Kaboom releases them. `--verify` plays every solution to confirm the level can be beaten.

## Source layout

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `main.swift` | App shell, fullscreen window, input, command-line tools entry |
| `Renderer.swift`, `Shaders.swift` | Metal multi-pass renderer and all shaders |
| `Atlas.swift`, `Font.swift` | Procedural sprite atlas, supersampled hog painter, CoreText font atlas |
| `Terrain.swift` | Destructible pixel terrain and its GPU data texture |
| `Game.swift` | Hog state machine, skills, simulation (30 ticks per second), screens, input |
| `GameDraw.swift` | World, HUD, splash, title, level select, briefing and results drawing |
| `Levels.swift`, `LevelGen.swift` | The 8 hand-made levels and the generator for the other 93 |
| `Synth.swift`, `Audio.swift` | Synthesizer, composer, voices, ambience, and the AVAudioEngine mixer |
| `Tools.swift` | Icon painter, simulator, level verifier, snapshots |
